Before you use any tool

Never enter identifiable patient, staff, learner or beneficiary data into a personal account, free or paid. Check every output against a source you trust: an email needs a glance, a dose needs a source. General AI assistants are not medical devices.

Prices and features change often; most were checked October to November 2026. Inclusion is not an endorsement. Full details, prompts and safety guidance are in the AI Toolkit for Acute Care Teams.
